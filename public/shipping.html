Shipping & Delivery Policy

Armoured, tracked, and insured.

All consignments are dispatched from our studio in India and shipped in protective outer cartons. Every order is fully insured until delivery.

1. Dispatch timelines

Order typeDispatch
Prepaid (UPI / card / netbanking)Within 24–48 hours of payment confirmation
Cash on DeliveryWithin 24–48 hours of order confirmation
Orders placed on Sunday or a public holidayDispatched the next working day

2. Delivery timelines

  • Metro cities: 2–4 business days after dispatch.
  • Rest of India: 3–7 business days after dispatch.
  • Remote / non-serviceable pin codes: 7–10 business days, or a full refund if we cannot deliver.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Weather, courier disruptions and regional restrictions can extend them. You receive a tracking link by email and WhatsApp as soon as the AWB is generated.

3. Shipping charges

  • Complimentary standard shipping on orders of ₹3,000 and above.
  • Flat ₹150 for orders below ₹3,000.
  • Cash on Delivery orders may carry a small handling fee, shown at checkout before you pay.

4. Serviceability

We currently deliver within India only. We do not ship to international addresses through this storefront. If your pin code is not serviceable, we will contact you and refund the full amount, including any fees.

5. Delivery attempts

Couriers make up to three delivery attempts. Please keep your phone reachable. A consignment returned as undelivered, refused, or unclaimed is refunded less the forward and return freight, and less the COD fee where applicable.

Damage in transit. Report it within 48 hours of delivery with photographs of the outer carton and the seal. Approved claims are replaced at our cost. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
